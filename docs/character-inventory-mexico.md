# Mexico's characters: who is shallow or Wikipedia (inventory, 2026-11-16)

Josh, 2026-11-16, after Don Anselmo ("it was wikipedia in a shrimp captains background") and Andrés ("May need a rewrite"): "scan mexico for similar shallow/wikipedia people - just inventory no changes". Nothing here changes a character. Claude read all 165 backgrounds (`here` in `content/es-mx/cities/*.ts`) and sorted them by hand; the sorting is a judgement, and the borderline ones could sit one group over.

## The groups

- **Wikipedia in a person** (36): most of the background is the place's history (dates, founders, UNESCO), one sentence or none is the person's, and it isn't theirs to say. Don Anselmo and Andrés (before his rewrite) are the type.
- **History that fits the job, no life** (15): guides, teachers, historians, restorers, sacristans who are asked. The history is theirs to say, but there is no family, past, taste or opinion.
- **Shallow: the job and nothing else** (32): two sentences about what they do. Not Wikipedia; just thin.
- **Trade knowledge in their hands, no life** (44): the food, drink, craft or music they work with, as they'd explain it. Fine as knowledge; still no life.
- **Has a life** (33): a family, a past, where they came from, an opinion, something on today. The model to copy.
- Hidden or no background: 5 (the hidden bakeries and one hidden sweet shop).

## What stands out

- **The first five cities are the thin ones.** Mexico City, Guadalajara, Oaxaca, Puebla and San Miguel de Allende hold nearly all the shallow backgrounds and almost no one with a life. The nine newer cities were written longer; most of the people with a life are there.
- **San Miguel de Allende pastes the same four facts into person after person:** Ignacio Allende was born here (8 of 14 backgrounds), UNESCO 2008 (5), Zeferino Gutiérrez designed the Parroquia (4), the Alborada fireworks (4). A cantinero, a barista, a shopkeeper, a taquero and a sweet seller all carry them.
- **In the newer cities the Wikipedia cases are people at sights:** a plaza, a theatre, a lighthouse, a murals stair. People with a trade there are fine.
- **The best backgrounds tell the history as the family's story.** Doña Eulalia (Zacatecas): "Your great-grandmother hid in a cellar that day and told the story all her life." Don Eleazar (Monterrey) worked at the steelworks that closed in 1986. Don Fermín (Tijuana) came from Michoacán in 1968 meaning to cross. Leydi (Mérida): her great-grandfather fought in the Caste War. Same facts as a plaque, but theirs.
- **Almost nobody, even in the last group, has tastes, an opinion about today, or something they are doing this week.** Rolando (Monterrey; the Rayados, "codos") and Brenda (Mazatlán; the trip she is proud of) are the nearest.
- **Memo, the best visit of 2026-11-16, is in the trade group.** His mother and the metate were made up in the visit, not written in his background.
