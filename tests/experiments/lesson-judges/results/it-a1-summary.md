Five judges (docs/lesson-judges.md), each a separate agent, on the 21 lessons in content/it/lessons.json (231 Italian lines).

| Judge | Failures | Where |
|---|---|---|

| Translation | 27 pairs | British English (holiday, have you got, biscuits, madam), notes inside the English, word-for-word English; one real error: *fresco* as "fresh" (cold) |

Three judges independently caught *Come dici?*. Failures cluster in two places the writing instructions control: the practice questions, and one lesson's scope. So by the bounded process, the writing instructions (content/it/prompts/lesson-steps.md) change first, before Intermediate lessons are written.
