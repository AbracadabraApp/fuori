Rewrite one character's "here" line. The line is the only thing the game tells Claude about who this person is today; Claude plays the character from it.

The rule (agreed with Josh, 2026-11-08, from C4 to C11): say what the person does and how, from their own point of view, with the specific details of their work that a newcomer could ask about. Keep it two or three sentences and under 60 words, in the second person ("You ...").

Leave out:
- encyclopedia facts and definitions about the place, its history or its dishes (the place's name, kind and neighbourhood are given to the character separately);
- the day, the time or the weather ("on Sundays", "today", "in the evening");
- steering about the learner ("for people just starting out", "gifts", "pricier");
- traits the character already has (their personality is given separately).

Keep: how their work actually goes, in specific details a newcomer could ask about. Start from the current line: keep its details about their work, and add only the ordinary steps of the job. Invent nothing a person in this role there might not do: no numbers, no staff, no history, no places or dishes the current line doesn't name.

Input: the character sheet, the place, and the current line. Output: only the new line.
