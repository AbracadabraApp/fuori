# App icon

**Chosen (Josh, 2026-10-17): `woodcut-1-relit/3`**, in the app as `ios/Fuori/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png` (1024 px, no transparency). Full-size original: `chosen-original.jpg`. Why: as an icon the flat colour fields work at home-screen size, and it's unique (Josh).

How it was reached (all in Grok, `grok-imagine-image-2.0`; each folder has its `prompt.txt`; candidates kept at 512 px):
1. `take1/`: folk painting of a lane up to a church. Good style, but the church made it look religious (Josh), and at 60 px the church was a speck.
2. `take2/`: the same style, a lane with washing across it. The washing reads at 60 px.
3. `woodcut/`: Josh's own woodcut of the washing lane with sea and hills (`source-woodcut.jpg`, from `source-painting.jpg`), made square by image edit, plus a straight crop (5).
4. `woodcut-square/`: Josh's woodcut prompt, drawn square from the start; 1 and 2 strongest. Dusky yellow sky.
5. `woodcut-bright/`: the prompt rewritten for midday light; lighter, less striking.
6. `woodcut-brighter-edit/`: square woodcut 2 relit by image edit (`prompt.txt`).
7. `woodcut-1-relit/`: square woodcut 1 (`0`) relit with the same edit; **3** chosen.
