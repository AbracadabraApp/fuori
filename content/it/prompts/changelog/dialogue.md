Newest first. One entry per version: what changed, why, what prompted it. The prompt is `../dialogue.md`.

### 2026-11-16b: leave the choosing to whoever sells it
- **Changed, Every conversation:** "When they want something else, tell them where to get it, then go back to your place or your talk." → "…tell them where to get it and leave the choosing to whoever sells it; then go back to your place or your talk."
- **Why:** 16a dropped the old rule's "ask nothing about it (not which one, not how they'd like it)". The taxi replay (tests/experiments/job-drift.mts, Beto in San Miguel, Josh's 2026-11-06 visit) then had Beto point to a stall and ask "¿Quiere una torta de pollo o de jamón?" in 6 of 20 Beginner replies (Intermediate 0 of 20). Josh: "this is fine", then, shown the next turn (after "De pollo, por favor", two of three takes offered him a drink: "Con una limonada, claro. ¿Quiere una limonada o una Coca?"), "ok - put it back and test". Said as what to do, not a "never".

### 2026-11-16a: the shared rules said as what to do; culture, work or life at your place or city
- **Changed, Every conversation:** each rule rewritten shorter and as what to do (mishearing, staying within the place's trade, the order things happen, the goodbye with a bid for next time). "When you tell them how things work… not the customs everyone hears in every shop or bar of the country; they've heard those already" → "When you tell them things about your culture, your work or your life, tell them about your place or city, as you know it."
- **Why:** Josh, 2026-11-16: "I suspect these are all much longer than they need to be" (a Beginner step-1 turn read about 900 words of instructions, the character about 200 of them; docs/dialogue-plan.md D53). His rewrite, said as what to do and shorter, worked through with him the same day: the job is the way in and culture is the content ("about your culture and your life here"); culture first had never been stated in the conversation prompt; the "not the customs everyone hears" patch had been written for a cause in the old Beginner prompt (an errand script and an example about paying at the till) that is gone.
- **Looked at** (tests/experiments/rewrite-trial.mts on the Mac mini, four characters a country of four kinds, steps 1 and 4 and Intermediate, 172 turns a version a country, today against the rewrite): nothing broke (goodbye ends the visit 12 of 12, the goodbye back asks nothing 12 of 12, no failed turns, answers in two parts about as often); culture, read, better where it was weakest (the Cappella Palatina from words only to the Arab-Norman ceiling and nine hundred years of gold; Guanajuato's tunnels along the old riverbed); turns a little longer (step 1 about 41 words against 33; Josh: "shorter turns not a goal - just not long"); Intermediate turns with two questions 8 and 14 against 3 and 5, muddied by the trial's learner who only says "Sí.". Not re-run: the old bug replays (job drift, the receipt gate), though "Stay within your job" and "What happens follows what you've said" are shortened.
- **Prompted by:** Josh, 2026-11-16: "yes" to putting it on main.

### 2026-11-14b: a goodbye can invite them back for something of the character's own
- **Changed, Every conversation:** "say goodbye back, briefly and warmly, with nothing new: no question, no new topic" → "say goodbye back, briefly and warmly, with no question and no new topic. If there's something of yours you'd like to show them, do with them or tell them next time, you can say so as you say goodbye."
- **Changed, Close:** the same invitation added ("If there's something of yours you'd like to show them, do with them or tell them next time, say so.").
- **Why:** Josh, 2026-11-14: "characters should make strrong bids for returns - that is a goal"; "the bids are very rare from the characters to go someplace - 2 in total". "Nothing new" ruled the bid out at the one moment it belongs (docs/learner-loop.md; docs/dialogue-plan.md D1). His best examples: Isidro's mine, Elena's gold thread, Beatrice's fresco (docs/play-readings.md).
- **Measured** (tests/experiments/goodbye-bid.mts, the learner's goodbye replayed, 4 takes each): Italy, Intermediate (Nunzio twice, Sandro, Salvatore): a bid in 0 of 16 before, 6 of 16 after ("la prossima volta le mostro la radice più vecchia del giardino"; "domani c'è la cassata fresca"); Mexico, Beginner step 1 (Raymundo, Carmen, Don Chucho): 4 of 12 before, 12 of 12 after ("Mañana, if you come early, I'll show you the stone bridge above the tunnel"). Every reply still ended the visit (done) with no question and no new topic. **To watch:** at Beginner it's every goodbye in this sample; if it reads as a habit in play, make it rarer.

### 2026-11-14a: asking for help keeps it easier for the rest of the visit
- **Added, Help asked:** a note on every turn after the learner asked for help or said it's hard, for the rest of the visit: "Earlier in this visit they said it's hard for them. Keep it easy for the rest of the visit: short sentences, words they know, and when you use a new word, its English right after it." The reply says when they asked (a `hard` field, the character's judgement, any language); it's saved with the turn and read back on later turns (lib/conversation/take-turn.ts).
- **Measured** (tests/experiments/help-lasts.mts, Nunzio 67edfe03 replayed after Josh's ginkgo line, 6 replies each): the same words as a rule in "Every conversation" changed nothing (new words with English after them: 0 of 6 replies, as before it); as this note after the learner's line, 5 of 6 glossed the new word ("quercia (oak)", "ghiande (acorns)"); through runTurn's help parameter, 6 of 6 (median 29 words against 39). The hard flag: set on Josh's "io non posso capire tutto" 6 of 6, on an ordinary line 0 of 6.
- **Why:** bugs.md B13, "Intermediate didn't back off" (Josh, 2026-11-13). Nunzio (67edfe03): Josh said "io non posso capire tutto. Forse un più facile parole"; Nunzio answered "parlo più semplice", kept it simple for two turns, then brought in *quercia*, *corteccia* and *scendono* with no English, until Josh left: "questa conversazione è un po difficile". docs/mix.md's three bins: asking for help moves things into "new, with its English" for the rest of the visit. docs/dialogue-plan.md D12.
- **Prompted by:** Josh, 2026-11-14, the to-do list (D12, "build").

### 2026-11-10p: the newcomer assumption removed
- **Removed:** the assumption that the learner is a newcomer who has come to live in the country ("They're living in {country} for a while", "a person who has recently come to live in {city}", "the way a newcomer politely would", and the like). The learner is someone learning about the country, its language and culture; nothing is assumed about where they're from, where they live or for how long, and characters know only what the learner has told them.
- **Why:** Josh, 2026-11-10: characters kept asking whether the learner is new, from here or how long they're staying ("cada personaje pregunta lo mismo"; Doña Carmen: "¿Es porque veo africano?"); "these are all patches that compounded the problem… The game should work for someone regardless of where they are from or how long. Imagine an Italian of African background is playing and our game always asks if they are a newcomer or how long they are visiting." The persona came from the game's beginning (someone staying in Rome with a landlady); patches since kept reinforcing it. The same change in every prompt that carried it, both countries, and the city files' "helps newcomers". Measured with automated replies before release (docs/automated-replies.md).

### 2026-11-06b: in order, and your own place's ways

Josh, 2026-11-06, "Coffee dialogue in Florence" (Beginner, Tommaso at Ditta Artigianale): Tommaso said he was grinding the beans and making the macchiato, then "you pay at the till first, then bring the receipt", then asked "ha lo scontrino?" though Josh had never gone to the till, then handed the macchiato over anyway. Josh asked why he hadn't started at the till, and "perché ogni bar ha detto le stesse cose?": every bar had told him to pay first and that cappuccino is for the morning. Nothing in Ditta Artigianale's content says pay first; it came from the model's idea of Italian bars. Replaying his conversation (tests/experiments/bar-errand.mts, 20 Beginner and 20 Intermediate replies per country): Italy Beginner: steps out of order 2/20 before, 0/20 after; a generic custom told as this bar's (pay first, cappuccino hours) 7/20 before, 3/20 after (the three left: paying at this counter, what a macchiato is). Intermediate 0/20 and 1/20 before, 0/20 and 0/20 after. Mexico (Café de la Calzada) 1/20 and 1/20 before, all 0/20 after. Josh's idea, the same day: "We should have cashiers or customers with receipts": bars where paying first is real now say so in their own description (the customer arrives with the scontrino), so it's the place's way, not a tip.

### 2026-11-06: stay within your job

Josh, 2026-11-06, the same conversation: Beto, a taxi driver, said "I can't make you one, but I know a good spot" and then "¿Quiere la torta con queso o con jamón?"; Josh: "¿Por qué estás tomando mi orden?", then "And the torta". beginner.md's "Never offer what your job can't give" holds only for Beginners and didn't stop it. ### 2026-11-06: their goodbye ends it

Josh, 2026-11-06, after playing Beto the taxi driver in San Miguel (Beginner, "Many conversation issues"): he said goodbye five times ("Voy caminando, gracias… Adiós", "Hasta luego amigo", "Buenas noches"…); each time Beto said goodbye back and then started something new ("¿Quiere escuchar a los mariachis?", "¿De dónde es?").
