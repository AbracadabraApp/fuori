### 2026-11-13b: step 1 writes the language as {piece | English}, put together by code
- **Changed, Level 1:** "Write every piece of {language} in braces with its English after a bar, like {pase, pase | come in}: the braces come out before it's said… Every piece, thanks and goodbyes too; in the English, leave out names said in the piece", and the closing question in braces with its English; the whole-turn example in the shape. The code puts the line together, the piece then its English (lib/dialogue/pieces.ts), as it streams (the voice starts on the first sentence) and on the whole line; English that only repeats the piece (a name) is dropped.
- **Why:** Josh, 2026-11-13: "I bigger issue is the runs of spanish without English support". Replayed in Mexico on five of his 11-12 evening visits (40 turns, unsupported-runs.mts): a run of 3+ words with no English in 62% of his real turns, 46% on 13a, 41% with the best wording; 15% with the shape (median 40 words, 24% Spanish), every piece in braces glossed. Most of the 15% was goodbyes, hence "thanks and goodbyes too". Each piece said once with its English (Dodson's repeat, Spanish again after the English, not chosen).
- **Prompted by:** docs/mix.md, Josh's "go", 2026-11-13.

### 2026-11-13a: step 1 mostly English; the limit on the language, not on the English
- **Changed, Level 1:** "you mix English and {language}… Your English is only there to carry the {language}" → "they follow English, and catch a phrase or two of {language}… you mix English and {language} the way you would with a visitor: you talk in English and put the {language} in… One or two of them a turn; everything else you say is in English, and short: no stories or explanations of your own". - **Why:** 12b shortened step 1 by limiting the English, so turns came out about half in the language, where CLAUDE.md has the Beginner talking "mostly in English with the language slotted in". Measured on Josh's Beginner visits: about 70% English 11-10 to 11-11, 46–52% on 11-12. Characters did use more English when he asked (Ofelia 24% → 85%), but every turn started from the lower base. Replayed in Mexico on five of his 11-12 evening visits (40 turns; Italy takes the same text, tests/experiments/b2-turn-length.mts): 12i 45% in Spanish, median 37 words; this text 32%, 39 words. Variants measured the same way: without "mix English and {language}" 25% (41 words; dropping the word moved it most, but tests/check-beginner-2.ts asks for it); also without "and short: no stories…" 23% (44 words); a two-sentence text that only says "mostly in English, with only a little {language}" (Josh's idea that a level could say just how much) 30% (42 words), but its {language} was nearly all the closing question, with no phrases slotted in. The learner hears fewer words of the language per turn (about 17 → 12): the cost of the change.
- **Prompted by:** Josh, 2026-11-13: "Need to get mixed dialogue working better"; "but how does a character respond and use more, but we haven't been able to get the base level recalibrated"; "make thee change after judging it empically - then I will review".

### 2026-11-12d: a new kind of question at steps 1–3, Notice
- **Added:** "Setup: notice" ("point them at something they can see right now and ask whether they see it or what they make of it, and give them two short answers they could say back"), drawn in the rotation at steps 1–3 beside two answers and questions to ask (lib/beginner-2.ts: rung `notice`, weight 2 against two answers' 3 and questions to ask's 1; never the same three times running, as before).
