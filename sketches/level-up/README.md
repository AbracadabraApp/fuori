# Level up: the approved mockups (Josh, 2026-11-10; exported 2026-11-11)

Two artboards from the claude.ai canvas "After-conversation page" (artifact J8E48kaJyscahyhGebkH8t), exported as standalone HTML and saved here as the design reference for the level-up screen (docs/beginner-2-step-2.md → The step up; docs/dialogue-structure-reference.md § 12.2; docs/glossary.md → Level up). The values to replicate are the inline styles; the markup isn't production code. The export's runtime (`support.js`, `vendor/react*.js`) is left out: the files read as plain HTML with inline styles.

- `level-up-mexico.html`: Ximena, Café Amapola, Ciudad de México. Papel picado band across the top (seven pennants in the palette's magenta, yellow, teal, green, terracotta, purple), the colibrí at the foot.
- `level-up-italy.html`: Luisa, Bar Mattina, Roma. A green-and-cream awning across the top with a terracotta rail, lemons on a branch at the foot.

Shared structure, top to bottom: the decoration band (96 pt); the character's initial in a sand circle, name (Alegreya 700, 17) and "Place · City" (13, muted); a coloured kicker "You're doing great with {name}" (14, 700; magenta in Mexico, green in Italy); the headline "Ready for more?" (Alegreya 800, 36/40); the label "HOW {NAME} TALKS TO YOU" (12, 700, letter-spaced, muted); two cards each with a 44 pt play button and a label: **Now** (white card, paper play button, her last line as said) and **Next step** (yellow card #ffe8a3 with a #f2a900 border and play button, the same line at the next step); the answers line "Your answers: still two to choose from, and now you can say your own." (15/21, #555); the yes button (54 pt pill, green #566330, white 18/700, in the language: "¡Sí, más español!" / "Sì, più italiano!"); "Not yet" (Alegreya 500, 26, underlined, #3f4a22); the decoration at the foot.

Review notes kept with the approval (docs/after-conversation.md): the decorations are flat vector where the game is watercolour and ink (redraw in the sketchbook hand, or accept these pages as a poster register); the magenta kicker on cream is at the contrast edge, one shade darker in the build. The yellow card and its #f2a900 aren't in design/tokens.json yet; add tokens before building.
